How Signals finds links

Signals holds books, artworks and musicians from collections across cultures.

Shared tags
Two works carry the same subject tags from their catalogs. Exact and explainable, but it misses links that are worded differently.
Meaning
Each work's description is turned into a point in a space of meaning by a small language model; nearby points are related even when no words match.
Blend
Both together: 40% shared tags, 60% meaning. The default.

Bridges are the strongest links to a different art form: a novel to a painting, a painting to a musician. Close kin are the nearest works of the same kind. Scores run from 0 to 1; links below a floor are never drawn.

The catalogs carry their own blind spots. Some collections are thin, and catalog tags reflect who did the cataloging. Signals shows what the data supports, and says why every link exists.
